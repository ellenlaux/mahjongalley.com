Which one fits you

You want everything in one app, free: Mahjong Alley

Learn from zero, practice solo, and play with your group online, all in one app with no ads and nothing to buy. None of the other apps here lists async tables, which let friends in different time zones, or with different schedules, keep one game going all week. iPhone and iPad only.

You practice alone, often offline: American Mahjong Practice

The longest-running American Mahjong practice app, with the most reviews, years of past hands, and offline play. No online play with friends.

You play several styles of mahjong, on any device: Mahjong 4 Friends

American plus Chinese and other styles, on phones, tablets and the web, with public tables when your friends aren't around.

You want to talk while you play: Real Mah Jongg or I Love Mahj

Real Mah Jongg has voice and text chat. I Love Mahj runs in a browser with video calls and scheduled group games. Both are subscriptions.

You're brand new and want structured lessons: Mahjong Alley or MAHJ

Mahjong Alley's lessons are free and lead straight into practice games and tables with friends. MAHJ has 18 lessons and drills for a one-time $4.99, and scorekeeping for your in-person game.

What to look for in an American Mahjong app

  • Real rules. The Charleston, joker limits, exposures that can't be taken back, concealed hands and dead hands. An app that bends these teaches habits you'll have to unlearn at a real table.
  • This year's hands. The card changes every spring. Check that the app is on the current card, and what it costs to get it.
  • The true price. Free downloads often mean ads, a daily game limit, or a subscription for the current card or for playing with friends.
  • How your group plays. Real-time tables need everyone free at once. Async tables don't.
  • Your devices. If someone in your group is on Android or a laptop, check that the app runs there.

Whatever app you use, a real table still needs everyone's own official card, sold by the National Mah Jongg League.
