What's inside ↓

American Mahjong, the way it's played at the table

Mahjong Alley is American Mahjong for iPhone and iPad, played on the 2026 card. You get all 152 tiles, a real Charleston, jokers, calling and exposures, and scoring. Play by standard rules, or switch on House Rules for blanks. It's free, with no ads and no subscription.

Questions people ask

Is Mahjong Alley free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and nothing to buy.

Is it American Mahjong or Chinese mahjong?

American Mahjong, with the Charleston, jokers and a yearly card of hands. It isn't Chinese, Hong Kong or Japanese mahjong, and it isn't a tile-matching solitaire game.

Can I play with friends who live somewhere else?

Yes. Host a table, send the link or the code, and play online. Tables can be live, or async so everyone takes their turn when it suits them. How tables work.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Mahjong Alley is on iPhone and iPad.
